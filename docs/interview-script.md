# JTBD Interview Script — Caretta Gazipaşa Volunteers

Goal: validate the three personas in `docs/research.md` §6 (Dawn Patroller, Coordinator, Night Excavator) before locking V1 scope. Run as a 20-30 minute conversation, in person or by call, with 5-8 real volunteers/coordinators spanning all three roles. Record answers verbatim where possible — the exact phrasing is often the best copy for onboarding/UI text later.

## Warm-up (2 min)

1. How long have you been volunteering with Caretta Gazipaşa, and what's your usual role — patrol, excavation, coordination, something else?
2. Walk me through the last time you were out on the beach. What did that morning/night actually look like, start to finish?

## Situation & current tool (5 min)

3. When you find a fresh crawl, what do you do first? How do you decide "nest" vs. "false crawl" — what are you actually looking at?
4. What do you write down, and on what (paper, phone notes, memory until later)? Who sees that data next, and when?
5. Has a nest or a crawl ever been misjudged, lost, or double-counted? What happened, and what would have prevented it?

## Job-to-be-done (8 min) — ask per role, using the "When I... I want to... so I can..." frame

**Dawn Patroller**
6. "When I find a fresh crawl at first light, I want to ___, so I can ___." — fill in your own words. What's the biggest thing slowing you down in that moment?
7. What happens if you're not sure whether something is a nest? Who do you ask, and how long does that take?

**Coordinator**
8. "When the season ends (or a nest finishes), I want to ___, so I can ___." What does your season-end reporting actually look like today — to whom, in what format?
9. What's the most common data-quality problem you deal with from volunteer submissions? How do you catch it, and how much time does fixing it cost you?

**Night Excavator**
10. "When I excavate a hatched nest, I want to ___, so I can ___." Walk me through how you currently count and calculate hatch success — by hand, on paper, later at home?
11. What's it like doing this at night — light, fatigue, phone battery, wet hands? What would make it meaningfully easier without disturbing the turtles or ruining your night vision?

## Motivation & retention (3 min)

12. What keeps you coming back to volunteer, season after season? (Don't lead — let them answer before naming things like "helping turtles," "community," "data/science.")
13. Would you want to see your own personal stats (nests found, patrols walked, hatchlings that made it to the sea)? What about seeing how your team/beach zone is doing?
14. How would you feel about a leaderboard ranking volunteers against each other? (Listen for discomfort/competitiveness — this validates or challenges the "no public leaderboard" recommendation in the research.)

## Tech reality check (2 min)

15. What phone do you carry on patrol, and does it usually have signal on the beach?
16. Have you used any app or GPS tool for this before (ArcGIS, Google Maps, anything)? What was that like?

## Closing (1 min)

17. If you could wave a magic wand and fix one thing about how data gets collected today, what would it be?
18. Anything I didn't ask that I should have?

## After the interview

- Log direct quotes against the persona they map to in `docs/research.md` §6.
- Flag any answer that contradicts a research assumption (especially Q14 on leaderboards, and Q3/Q5 on false-crawl misjudgment) — these are the highest-value corrections to make before locking V1 scope.
