**Description** (≤4000):
Caretta Friends is a cute, field-ready app for volunteers who protect loggerhead sea-turtle (Caretta caretta) nests — from the crawl at dawn to the night the hatchlings reach the sea.

Made for Caretta Gazipaşa on Türkiye's protected nesting beaches, and designed to work for any sea-turtle volunteer community.

WHAT IT DOES
• Shared map of every nest, beach and trash spot — mark a nest in seconds with a photo (auto-geotagged).
• AR nest finder — point your phone and see nests pinned where they physically are.
• Nest lifecycle — countdown to hatch, a timeline of updates, and an honest temperature-based sex-prediction range.
• Excavation record — hatching % and emergence success, exported as a PDF for the local authority.
• Field guides & facts — what to do when you find a nest, no lights at night, beach rules — in your language.
• Patrols & coverage — so volunteers don't re-walk a covered stretch.
• Your impact — see how many hatchlings you helped reach the sea, keep a streak, earn badges.

Offline-first — capture works with no signal and syncs when you're back online. Works without an account.
