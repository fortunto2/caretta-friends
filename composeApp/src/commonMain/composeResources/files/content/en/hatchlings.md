# Helping hatchlings out

Hatchlings emerge at night and **must reach the sea on their own** — that's how they imprint on their beach and return years later.

- Don't pick them up or carry them to the water unnecessarily.
- If one is stuck during excavation or misled by light, help it find the **direction** to the sea, but let it crawl itself.
- Clear obstacles from its path and **turn off all lights**.
