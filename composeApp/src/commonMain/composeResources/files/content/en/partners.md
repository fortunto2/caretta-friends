# Conservation partners & contacts

Groups, university centers and official bodies that protect sea turtles on Türkiye's nesting beaches. Many aren't on Caretta Friends yet — reach them directly, and if you run one of them, message us and we'll give you access.

**🚨 Sick, injured or dead turtle?** Call **DEKAMER emergency +90 538 403 2501**, or **112**.
