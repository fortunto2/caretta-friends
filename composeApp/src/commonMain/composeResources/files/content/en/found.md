# Found a nest? What to do

Spotted a fresh turtle track or a nest? **Don't touch the eggs and don't dig.**

- Mark the spot in the app with a **photo** and **GPS**, then drop a marker.
- Tell the **beach leader**.
- If someone is disturbing it, or it's threatened by people or animals, stay nearby at a distance, don't draw attention, and call the community or the gendarmerie (**155**).

Remember: until a nest is fenced and capped with a cage, it's only a *signal* that eggs were laid here.
