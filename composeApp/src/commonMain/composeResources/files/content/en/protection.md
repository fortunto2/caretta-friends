# How we protect nests

Finding a nest is only half the job. To protect the clutch, volunteers:

- set **stakes and reed** around it,
- run **tape**,
- attach a **warning sign** for tourists,
- and cap it with a protective **grid or cage** against predators and people.

A nest counts as **protected only after the cage is installed**. Before that, it's just a signal that eggs were laid here. Record in the app exactly what has been installed.
