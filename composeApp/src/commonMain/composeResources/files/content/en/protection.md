# How we protect nests

Finding a nest is only half the job — the clutch still has to be protected. There are **two ways** to protect a nest, and **either one counts as "protected"**:

## 🌾 Reed fence — the everyday option
- Drive **wooden stakes** in a square around the nest,
- run **reed / cane fencing** and **warning tape** between them,
- add a **warning sign** so people and vehicles stay off it.

Light, cheap, made from on-hand materials and fast to set up — this is what we use for most nests, because there are many nests and few cages.

## 🛡️ Metal cage — the strong option
A metal wire cage over the nest does the same job **plus** stops foxes and dogs from digging up the eggs. Cages are **heavy to carry and in short supply**, so we keep them for predator-prone sites. (Buying more cages is a goal for the future.)

In the app, record exactly what you installed — **Reed** or **Cage**. Both keep the nest safe.
