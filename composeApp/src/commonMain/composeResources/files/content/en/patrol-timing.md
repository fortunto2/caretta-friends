# When to patrol

The best patrol is an **early-morning** one.

**Right after 08:00** (when the access ban lifts on protected beaches) is ideal:

- **Fresh tracks** — the night's crawls and new nests are still clear in the sand before the wind and footprints erase them.
- **Low UV, cool sand** — kinder to you, and to any hatchlings you might handle.
- **Calm light** — no harsh midday sun.

**Avoid midday (11:00–16:00)** — high UV, heat stress, and the sand is too hot to work comfortably.

An **evening** walk before **20:00** works too — but on protected beaches access is **banned 20:00–08:00 (1 May – end of Sep)**, so never patrol into the night without authorization.

Before you head out, **tap the weather pill on the map**:

- ☀️ **UV high** → go early or late, wear a hat, hydrate.
- 🌫️ **Dust high** → patrol is **not advised** — reschedule.

> No flash near nests at dawn or dusk. Keep the shore dark.
